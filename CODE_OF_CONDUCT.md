# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Contributors must communicate with
professionalism, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and focus discussions on the
project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publishing
private information without permission, and sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and applied consistently.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a
public issue containing personal or sensitive information. Reports will be reviewed confidentially
and without retaliation against a good-faith reporter.
